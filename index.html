About Bakes by Z

Founded in 2015 by master baker Zara, Bakes by Z brings artisanal cake craftsmanship to homes across the UK. What started as a small home bakery has grown into a beloved local business known for exceptional quality and creative designs.

Every cake is crafted using the finest ingredients, with attention to detail that makes each creation unique. We believe cakes should not only taste delicious but also be beautiful works of art that make your celebrations unforgettable.
